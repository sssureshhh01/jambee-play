Jambee — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Jambee is made by Suresh & Vidya ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Jambee.

What we collect

Nothing. Jambee does not ask for an account, your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Game progress

Your scores, coins, levels and settings are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The current version of Jambee shows no ads. If we add ads in a future update, this policy will be updated first to explain what the ad provider collects.

Children

Jambee does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
