Jotshot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Jotshot is a document scanner and PDF tool made by Suresh & Vidya ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Jotshot.

Your documents stay on your phone

Everything you scan, import, compress, convert, merge or read text from is processed on your own device. Your documents, photos and the text read from them are never uploaded to us or to any server. They are stored in Jotshot's private storage on your phone until you save, share or delete them. Uninstalling Jotshot deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. Jotshot has no account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers or analytics.

Permissions

Jotshot does not request camera, storage, contacts, location or microphone permissions. The scanner runs inside Google Play services, and you choose each file or photo yourself through Android's file and photo pickers. Internet access is used only to show ads.

Google services used inside the app

  • Google ML Kit (document scanner and text recognition) runs on your device. Your images and text are not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical information such as device type, app version and performance metrics to keep the service working.
  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version of Jotshot. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about how ads are shown and tapped, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Jotshot requests non-personalised ads by default. Ads never have access to your documents. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings.

Sharing

A document only leaves Jotshot when you choose to save it to your phone's Downloads or Pictures, or share it to another app. What happens after that is controlled by the app you share it with.

Children

Jotshot is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
