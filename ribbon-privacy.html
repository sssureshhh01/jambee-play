Ribbon Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Ribbon Rush: Untangle Puzzle is made by Suresh & Vidya ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Ribbon Rush.

What we collect

We do not ask for an account, your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not collect any personal information ourselves.

Game progress

Your levels, stars, coins, pictures and settings are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

Ribbon Rush shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Ribbon Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
